The Instructor

Hi, I'm Alfred Zeo S.

I teach keyboard to students of every age, from complete beginners picking out their first notes to players working toward exams and performance pieces. Lessons are one-on-one and in person, built around how you learn best rather than a fixed syllabus.

"Every student starts at a different note — my job is to help you find your rhythm and stick with it."

Classes are held at Hennur Garden, Bengaluru, with a focus on steady, practical progress: proper technique, music reading, and real songs you'll actually enjoy playing.
